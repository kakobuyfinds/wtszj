What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

A SuperBuy spreadsheet is an organized collection of product leads used to compare items before ordering. A useful entry records the source link, selected variant, measurements and the date checked; it does not guarantee availability, quality or authenticity.

The four-check method

Look beyond the cover photo.

Use this sequence whenever two listings look alike. Each check resolves a different uncertainty.

01 / Identify

Save the exact variant

Record the source, color, size and material description. A shared image does not establish that two sellers offer the same item.

02 / Measure

Compare something you own

Use a well-fitting garment as your reference. Match measuring methods before comparing numbers.

03 / Inspect

Ask a specific question

A close view of a seam, fastening or label can answer more than another full-length picture.

04 / Budget

Price the complete journey

Keep the item cost separate from delivery, payment charges and other applicable costs. Recheck before paying.

Field notes / Four short articles

From a saved link to an informed choice.

SuperBuy finds, a SuperBuy sizing guide, SuperBuy QC photos and a SuperBuy shipping checklist: four topics to work through at your own pace.

01 / Product research

SuperBuy finds: build a shortlist that stays useful

Start with a purpose: a daily shoe, a layering piece or a bag that fits your essentials. A small set of comparable options is easier to evaluate than a long list of unrelated bookmarks.

  • How to use a SuperBuy spreadsheet: save the source and selected option together, then add a dated note about what you checked.
  • SuperBuy shoes spreadsheet: compare the model, intended use, size convention and outsole details.
  • SuperBuy hoodies spreadsheet: look for chest width, body length, fabric composition and care instructions.
  • SuperBuy T-shirt finds: compare shoulder width and length instead of judging fit from a styled photograph.
  • SuperBuy jackets spreadsheet: allow room for layers and examine cuffs, closures and lining.
  • SuperBuy product link not working: search again by product type and identifying details; reassess any replacement listing.
02 / Fit and materials

SuperBuy sizing guide: measure the garment, then choose

A familiar size letter can mean something different on another listing. Lay a garment flat, use the seller's measuring method and compare the exact variant you plan to select.

  • How to read a seller size chart: establish the unit and whether figures describe a body or a finished garment.
  • How to compare sneaker sizes: check the listing's sizing convention and any model-specific guidance.
  • Hoodie chest width measurement: compare a flat width with a flat width, not with a full circumference.
  • Pants and shorts measurement guide: check waist, rise, thigh and inseam together.
  • How to check jersey fit: measure chest and body length; fitted and relaxed cuts need different allowances.
  • Headwear sizing before ordering: compare head circumference with the adjustment range or fitted-size chart.
03 / Photo review

SuperBuy QC photos: turn a picture into a decision

A useful photo request names the uncertainty: a crooked seam, an unclear size label or a closure you cannot see. Where inspection photographs are available, review the exact item rather than another buyer's example.

  • What to check in QC photos: selected color, size label, visible condition and construction details.
  • How to request detail photos: name the area, viewing angle and measurement you need.
  • Seller photos versus inspection photos: promotional images show a presentation; inspection images should show the item being assessed.
  • How to inspect sneaker stitching: compare both shoes and ask about visible gaps, loose threads or asymmetry.
  • Accessory hardware inspection checklist: review fasteners, edges, attachments and any visible damage.
  • Can QC photos prove authenticity? no; photographs alone cannot establish provenance or authenticate a branded product.
04 / Budget and delivery

SuperBuy shipping checklist: plan beyond the item price

Make the budget before committing to the shortlist. Keep assumptions visible, because a parcel's final packaging and the available delivery options can change the total.

  • SuperBuy total cost checklist: separate item cost, domestic delivery, service fees, payment charges and international delivery.
  • How to estimate parcel weight: include packaging in your planning and confirm the final packed measurements.
  • Actual weight versus volumetric weight: ask which billing method and divisor apply to the quoted service.
  • International shipping quote comparison: compare inclusions, tracking, exclusions and the same destination.
  • Clothing parcel budget for Europe: check destination-specific import requirements through the relevant authority before ordering.
  • First haul planning checklist: record your budget, unresolved questions, return conditions and delivery constraints before paying.

Before you continue.

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

Is this an official SuperBuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide with no official association with SuperBuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Dior or other brands mentioned. Category links lead to HipobuyFind; all other navigation links lead to its homepage.

Do the images prove that an item is authentic?

No. The images are third-party listing references, not authentication evidence or product endorsements. For authenticated branded goods, use the brand or an authorized retailer and check provenance.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping?

Do not assume it does. Confirm the item price, domestic delivery, any service and payment fees, international shipping and destination charges separately with the relevant provider.

How should I compare sizes across listings?

Use the measurements for the exact listing and variant. Compare them with an item that fits you, checking units, measuring method and whether the chart gives garment or body measurements.

What should I do if a product link stops working?

Search the catalog again using the product type and identifying details. Check the new listing independently instead of assuming a similar photograph means the same seller, material or item.
